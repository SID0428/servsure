1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ELEVATOR PITCH
================================================================================
"ServSure is an enterprise-grade, multi-tenant Service Intelligence and After-Sales 
Operations Platform built specifically for home appliance manufacturers, authorized 
franchise service hubs, field technicians, and consumers.

In the consumer durables and home appliances sector, post-sales operations are 
notoriously broken: customers deal with fake warranty claims and unresponsive call centers, 
manufacturers leak millions in fraudulent spare parts and bogus warranty reimbursements, 
and local franchise centers struggle to dispatch technicians efficiently.

ServSure bridges this entire gap through a single, unified digital platform:
- Direct consumer appliance onboarding with invoice validation and OCR/audit gating.
- Brand manufacturer control over fiscal policies, spare parts catalogs, and franchise hubs.
- Dual-OTP cryptographic physical presence verification (start-job and complete-job).
- Serialized parts tracking from central depot to warehouse shelf to consumer appliance.
- Automated multi-tier SLA escalation background worker preventing breach of service.

The platform eliminates warranty fraud, slashes turnaround time (TAT) from days to 
hours, and provides real-time visibility across thousands of service calls."

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT & INDUSTRY CONTEXT
================================================================================
Post-purchase appliance servicing suffers from four systemic industry pain points:

1. Warranty Fraud & Document Forgery:
   Customers upload fake or altered invoices to claim free servicing on expired appliances.
   Brands lose 8-15% of annual service budgets on illegitimate repairs.

2. Inventory Shrinkage & Counterfeit Spares:
   Technicians replace costly parts (e.g., inverter compressors, BLDC motors, PCB boards) 
   with cheap unbranded aftermarket parts, while billing the brand OEM for authentic spares.

3. Field Job Verification Vacuum:
   Without proof of physical arrival and completion, technicians can falsely mark tickets 
   as "Customer Not Available" or close jobs without actual customer sign-off.

4. Unresponsive Regional Franchises:
   Tickets get stuck at regional franchise hubs without timely assignment, causing SLA breaches 
   with zero automated escalation to brand managers.

ServSure solves all four using cryptographic OTPs, serialized inventory lifecycle tracking, 
fiscal year policy snapshots, and an automated background SLA state machine.

5.1. End-to-End Service Request Life Cycle & Dual-OTP Security
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard Flow:
1. Customer books a service request:
   Selects registered appliance, complaint type (e.g. "Not Cooling"), preferred appointment date & slot.
   System generates unique Ticket ID (e.g. SR-7845).
   System auto-generates a cryptographic 4-digit start_otp (e.g. "4821").

2. Smart Regional Dispatch:
   The request is routed to the authorized franchise branch covering the customer's pincode.
   Franchise manager assigns an available technician based on specialty and current workload.

3. Step 1 Physical Arrival Verification (Start Job):
   Technician arrives at doorstep and requests the customer's start_otp.
   Technician submits the OTP via mobile portal:
   - System validates OTP.
   - Updates status from Assigned -> In Progress.
   - Records doorstep timestamp.
   *Eliminates fake "technician visited but customer unavailable" excuses.*

4. Step 2 Repair, Estimate & Job Card:
   Technician diagnoses appliance.
   If extra spare parts are needed, an Estimate or Job Card is created.
   Parts warranty coverage is evaluated against active policy terms.

5. Step 3 Physical Handover & Satisfaction Sign-Off (Complete Job):
   Upon completing repair, system generates a separate 4-digit completion_otp sent to customer.
   Technician enters the completion OTP, captures the customer's digital canvas signature, 
   and inputs the diagnostic report.
   Status moves to Completed.
   Customer receives an automated Brevo email invoice & service certificate.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2. Warranty Registration, Fiscal Policy Matching & Approval Gate
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*Latest Feature Enhancement (v2.3.2):*

1. Customer Appliance Onboarding:
   - Customer adds an appliance with brand, category, model number, serial number, 
     purchase date, and mandatory proof-of-purchase invoice upload.
   - Appliance is created with warranty_status = "Pending Verification", is_approved = False.

2. The Approval Gate (Zero Premature Visibility):
   - **Crucial Rule:** Until approved by the company, this customer does **NOT** appear in 
     the company's Customers directory. The appliance is strictly isolated.
   - The brand manufacturer receives a notification in their **OEM Warranty Audit Queue**.

3. Brand OEM Verification Audit:
   The company admin inspects the high-resolution invoice preview or PDF, serial number, and purchase date:

   - **Path A: Approve OEM Warranty**
     -> Warranty status is set to Active.
     -> is_approved = True, is_added = True.
     -> Policy resolution engine matches the purchase date against the active fiscal policy 
        window (e.g. FY25-26 Comprehensive Coverage) and captures an immutable JSON policy snapshot.
     -> The customer and appliance are added to the Company's Customers section.
     -> Customer receives an automated email certificate with coverage dates and policy details.

   - **Path B: Reject with Decision Prompt**
     The admin selects the rejection reason (e.g., blurry document, mismatched model, date expired).
     The dialog prompts: "Add customer & appliance to company directory?"
     
     -> **Choice 1: No, Do Not Add (Default)**
        - Status becomes Rejected - Not Added, is_approved = False, is_added = False.
        - Appliance is excluded from customer's active appliances catalog.
        - Customer is NOT added to the company directory.
        - Company Dashboard clearly displays: ✕ Rejected - Not Added with the company's explicit reason.
     
     -> **Choice 2: Yes, Add Customer & Appliance**
        - Status becomes Rejected, is_approved = True, is_added = True.
        - Appliance is added to customer's portal showing: ❌ Warranty Rejected: <company reason>.
        - Customer and appliance are added to Company Customer Directory (marked as out-of-warranty).
        - Company Dashboard displays: ⚠️ Warranty Rejected (Added) with the reason.

5.3. Spare Parts Catalog, Serialized Inventory & Requisitions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To eliminate counterfeit spare parts:
- Every high-value part (compressor, PCB, motor) has a unique serialized barcode (PartSerialNumberModel).
- Franchise branches submit stock requisitions to the Brand OEM.
- When the Brand fulfills the order, stock credits into the franchise warehouse.
- When a technician installs a part during a job card, they must scan or select the 
  exact in-stock serial number.
- Once assigned to a job card, that serial number is permanently linked to the customer's 
  appliance and marked installed in the database, preventing parts duplication or theft.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4. Digital Repair Estimates & Customer Approval Flow
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- If an appliance repair requires out-of-warranty components or labor charges exceeding ₹500:
  Technician creates a digital estimate via mobile portal.
- Ticket enters Estimate Pending Approval status.
- Customer receives an instant SMS/email and an interactive approval modal on their dashboard.
- Customer clicks "Approve Estimate" or "Reject Estimate":
  - If approved: Technician is authorized to consume parts and proceed with repair.
  - If rejected: Repair is paused, preventing customer billing disputes.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.5. AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts) Packages & Subscriptions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Manufacturers define AMC tiers: Silver (Basic Maintenance), Gold (Comprehensive), Platinum (Zero Cost Parts).
- Customers can subscribe directly from their dashboard.
- AMC subscriptions auto-extend service coverage dates and automatically waive labour visiting fees.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.6. Automated Multi-Tier SLA Escalation & Reassignment Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Background engine continuously audits open tickets against organizational SLAs:
  - Level 1 Escalation: Unassigned for > 2 hours -> Auto-alerts Franchise Hub Manager.
  - Level 2 Escalation: Unassigned / Stalled for > 6 hours -> Auto-reassigns to backup branch.
  - Level 3 Escalation: Delayed resolution for > 24 hours -> Direct incident alert to Brand OEM Director.
- Eliminates ticket stagnation and ensures transparent operational accountability.

6. SECURITY, SESSIONS & MULTI-TENANCY ISOLATION
================================================================================
- PBKDF2 Password Hashing:
  Uses hashlib.pbkdf2_hmac with 310,000 iterations and cryptographic 16-byte random salts.
- HMAC-SHA256 Signed Session Tokens:
  Tokens follow the format srv1.{user_id}.{expiry_timestamp}.{hmac_signature}.
  Prevents token tampering while maintaining stateless validation performance.
- Dual-Mode Token Transport:
  Compatible with standard HTTP Authorization headers (Bearer <token>) and secure HTTP-Only 
  cookies (servsure_user_id, servora_user_id), guaranteeing seamless multi-browser session persistence.
- Multi-Tenant Row-Level Security:
  All database queries filter strictly by company_id and authorized branch_id. One brand or 
  franchise can never view or manipulate another brand's customers, tickets, or spare parts.

8. DEEP-DIVE ENGINEERING HIGHLIGHTS & ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS
================================================================================

1. Why Vanilla JavaScript instead of React / Next.js for the Frontend?
   - Decision: The operations dashboards (Company, Franchise, Technician) require fast rendering, 
     low memory footprint on low-end technician smartphones, and zero hydration overhead.
   - Benefit: Instant sub-50ms paint times, zero compilation build step, and direct DOM manipulation 
     with shared utility libraries (ops-dashboard.js).

2. Startup Reflection Database Migrations (seeds.py):
   - In production cloud setups (like Aiven MySQL and Render containers), running Alembic 
     migrations during zero-downtime deploys often creates locking bottlenecks.
   - Decision: Built a dynamic reflection inspection engine using sqlalchemy.inspect(engine). 
     On container boot, it inspects existing columns, executes non-blocking ALTER TABLE ADD COLUMN 
     only if missing, and self-heals legacy rows with default values.

3. Deterministic Component Warranty Tag Matching vs String Fuzzy Matching:
   - Problem: Fuzzy matching string names like "Compressor Bracket" against "Compressor" led to 
     false positives, covering non-warranted accessories under OEM component warranty.
   - Decision: Developed an explicit warranty_component_type enumeration on SparePartModel. 
     The engine checks comprehensive date first, and only tests tag-based component extensions 
     if the exact component type is tagged on the physical inventory item.

4. Free-Tier Cloud Keep-Alive & SLA Background Worker:
   - Decision: Render free-tier instances sleep after 15 minutes of inactivity. Implemented an 
     internal ping and SLA engine that executes periodic health and audit tasks, keeping the service 
     responsive while checking for overdue service requests.

9. TOP 15 INTERVIEW QUESTIONS & MODEL ANSWERS (TECHNICAL & ARCHITECTURAL)
================================================================================

Q1: What is ServSure in one sentence?
A1: ServSure is a multi-tenant After-Sales Service Intelligence platform connecting appliance 
    manufacturers, franchise centers, technicians, and customers with serialized inventory 
    tracking and dual-OTP physical presence verification.

Q2: How do you prevent unauthorized brand access between different manufacturers?
A2: We enforce row-level tenant isolation in SQLAlchemy. Every query on appliances, tickets, 
    branches, and parts filters strictly on company_id. Even if a user knows another company's 
    UUID, the API dependency require_company() rejects the request with HTTP 403.

Q3: How does the new Warranty Approval Gate work?
A3: When a customer uploads an appliance with invoice, it is created with is_approved=False. 
    The /api/company/customers query strictly filters is_approved == True. Only after the 
    brand OEM audits the invoice and approves it (or rejects with add_customer=True) does 
    the customer enter the company's records.

Q4: Why use a 2-OTP verification model for field visits?
A4: Single OTP only proves the technician met the customer at some point. Dual OTP guarantees 
    both arrival time (Start OTP) and job satisfaction upon physical completion (Complete OTP). 
    This prevents technicians from closing tickets prematurely or claiming phantom visits.

Q5: How is sensitive data like user passwords secured?
A5: Passwords are encrypted using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and 310,000 hashing rounds with unique 
    16-byte random salts per user, exceeding OWASP recommended standards.

Q6: How do you handle PDF vs Image invoice verification in the browser?
A6: Our media handler detects MIME type. Images are rendered inline with zoomable previews, 
    while PDFs are served through an embedded viewer with a direct full-screen modal trigger.

Q7: What happens if a customer's comprehensive warranty is expired but their compressor fails?
A7: The evaluate_part_warranty_status() engine checks if the appliance purchase date falls within 
    the component warranty window for that specific part tag (e.g. 120 months for compressor). 
    If valid, the customer pays ₹0 for the part, and the system bills the OEM via a Warranty Claim.

Q8: How do you prevent double-spending or counterfeit parts in franchise hubs?
A8: High-value parts have individual serialized records (PartSerialNumberModel). When used in a 
    Job Card, the serial number is marked installed and bound to the appliance ID. It can never 
    be used or claimed again.

Q9: What is your API design philosophy for this project?
A9: RESTful endpoints grouped by domain routers (/api/auth, /api/company, /api/requests, 
    /api/warranty-amc), strict Pydantic request/response validation schemas, and predictable 
    HTTP status codes (200, 201, 400, 403, 404, 422).

Q10: How do you handle database migrations without downtime?
A10: In seeds.py, we query the database dialect schema on startup. If a column like is_approved 
     does not exist, we execute an ALTER TABLE ADD COLUMN with safe defaults, followed by data 
     backfill in a single atomic transaction.

Q11: How do you prevent SLA breaches when regional franchise hubs are overwhelmed?
A11: The background SLA engine audits ticket age. If a ticket sits unassigned beyond the configured 
     threshold (e.g. 2 hours), it triggers Level 1 escalation. If breached further, it auto-reassigns 
     the ticket to an adjacent branch within the same geographic zone.

Q12: Why did you pick FastAPI over Django or Flask?
A12: FastAPI provides native asynchronous performance, automatic interactive OpenAPI docs (/docs), 
     and Pydantic v2 data validation, which is 5x to 10x faster than Django REST Framework.

Q13: How is email delivery handled in production?
A13: We use the Brevo (Sendinblue) transactional REST API via Python urllib with timeout fallbacks, 
     sending rich HTML notifications for OTPs, warranty approvals, and service invoices.

Q14: How does the application scale if 10,000 customers book services simultaneously?
A14: The FastAPI backend is stateless; session tokens are HMAC-validated without database session locks. 
     The database uses connection pooling with SQLAlchemy. The frontend is cached on Vercel's Edge CDN.

Q15: If you had another 2 weeks, what would you add next?
A15: I would integrate WhatsApp Business API for instant OTP delivery and two-way technician 
     tracking links, plus an AI OCR model (e.g., Google Cloud Vision) to pre-read serial numbers 
     and dates from invoices automatically.
